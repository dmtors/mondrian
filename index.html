Mondrian

An instrument made of Wikipedia articles. Every choice sounds and looks different.

Octave

Tap or drag across the painting. On a keyboard, play A–L; − and + change the octave.

    How it works

    Four Wikipedia articles make the instrument. Each one shapes part of the sound.

    • Waveform: the article’s topic picks the tone. Science and technology are square, culture is sawtooth, geography is triangle, history and society are sine.
    • Filter: the more pages link to the article, the brighter the sound.
    • Envelope: the older the article, the longer each note rings.
    • Effect: the more it’s been edited this month, the more the note wobbles.

    Each rectangle is a note in the key, scale and octave you choose; in a pentatonic scale there are no wrong notes. The bigger the rectangle, the lower the note. The four article titles decide the arrangement and colours: the same articles always make the same painting.
